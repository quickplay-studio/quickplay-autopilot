# The pipeline as a service, later

Note for Charlie, 25 October 2026. Nothing here is public, priced or started.

## What it could be

A data-driven Shorts machine for other faceless creators: trends, then checked fact packs, then boards, render, upload, and a measurement layer that feeds the next day's choices (every Short is a test, and a verdict needs proof). Three shapes, safest first:

- **Measurement only:** our drivers, change ledger and hub pointed at their channel. It makes no content, so it carries the least policy risk.
- **Set-up:** we install and tune the pipeline on their own accounts, then hand it over.
- **Managed:** we run it for them for a monthly fee.

## Proof we need first

Either of these:

- A channel past 1,000 subscribers and 10 million Shorts views in 90 days (the Partner Programme bar).
- A clear lift chart: median views per Short at 24 hours, week by week, with each change from the ledger marked and its verdict. Clear means at least double between the first 4 weeks and the latest 4, a 90% interval above 1.0, both halves of the data agreeing, and no worse on dead Shorts or subscribers per 1,000 views.

Today we have neither: 12 subscribers and about 26,500 views in all.

## What it would cost us

Nothing but allowance. No paid tools, free keys only. The allowance is GitHub Actions minutes, free model quotas, YouTube's 10,000 units a day (shared by our four channels) and Charlie's Claude allowance. So a customer runs on their own accounts and keys, and we start with one.

## Risks

- **YouTube's inauthentic content rule** (mass-produced or repetitive content can lose monetisation) applies to every customer as it does to us. Each channel needs its own topics, facts, voice and look, sources cited, and nothing repeated across customers. We refuse volume without substance.
- **Our keys stay ours.** They are never shared with a customer, and theirs never go into our repository, so one customer's strike or ban cannot reach our channels.
- **No promised results.** Platforms change rules without notice (Meta limited our Facebook Page on 23 October).
- **Our channel rules travel with the code:** family-friendly, UK English, every claim sourced. A customer who wants politics, gambling or health claims is not a customer.
- **Paperwork:** contract, UK GDPR and tax need proper advice before the first paid customer.

## First step when the proof exists

One pilot for 28 days: a faceless creator we choose, a one-page written scope, their own accounts and keys. Measurement only for the first week to set the baseline, then the pipeline on half their Shorts so the lift is a fair comparison. The lift chart goes to Charlie before anything else happens.

## No public action now

No page, name, price, outreach or account until the proof exists and Charlie says yes.
