How to post one
  1. Tap Copy caption on the Short marked Next up.
  2. In TikTok, open Inbox, then System notifications, and tap that Short's draft.
  3. Tap Next, paste the caption into the description, then tap Post.
  4. Come back here and tap Posted.

TikTok holds at most 5 waiting drafts, so post each day's drafts that day, or the next day's can't arrive.
