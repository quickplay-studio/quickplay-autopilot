Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Quickplay Uploader is used only by the owner of the Quickplay Daily YouTube channel, to manage that channel. It has no other users.

What it accesses

  • Uploads and schedules videos on the owner's own YouTube channel (YouTube Data API, upload scope).
  • Reads the owner's own channel and video details, such as scheduled videos and titles (read-only scope).
  • Reads the owner's own YouTube Analytics reports, such as views and subscribers (read-only scope).

What it stores

The sign-in token is stored as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub settings. Channel statistics used for planning may be saved in the owner's repository. No data about any other person is collected, sold or shared.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time from Google account permissions.
