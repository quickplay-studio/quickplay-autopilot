Quickplay Uploader

Quickplay Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of Quickplay Daily. It publishes the channel's own short videos to the channel's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts, and reads the channel's own analytics to plan new videos.

It is not offered to other users and has no sign-ups.
